# ArguPath

**INT6065 · Group 13**

[Open the writing workshop](https://qwerty123269.github.io/argupath/) · [Download the complete project package](https://qwerty123269.github.io/argupath/ArguPath_Submission.zip)

ArguPath is a bilingual writing workshop for primary, secondary and university students. Writers select a sentence, examine it using a question suited to the genre, and save a revision with a reason for the change. The interface is available in English and Traditional Chinese.

## Start with the academic writing demo

Choose **University → Academic writing → Start academic demo · Step 1**.

The draft claims that social media use “consistently increases anxiety.” The word *consistently* raises one question: do the six studies agree? The verb *increases* raises another: do their designs support a causal conclusion?

Follow the guide to inspect the sources, compare study results and trace a claim through the evidence map. Then decide how to respond to feedback, revise the sentence and save the reason for the edit. A final task asks whether the same reasoning applies to a new research question. The demo uses prepared feedback and synthetic research data.

**To watch the walkthrough:** Open `Presentation/ArguPath_Group13.pptx` in the project package and play **slide 18, “Academic writing coaching demo”** (2 min 55 sec). The separately submitted video, `INT 6065 Group13 Project Demo.mp4`, contains the same walkthrough.

## Explore the school workshops

| Level | Genre | What the writer practises |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Story writing | Show how a child helps someone shelter a paper bird from the rain by describing a gesture the reader can picture. |
| Secondary | Narrative | Recreate a missed musical cue using details the narrator can see or hear. |
| Secondary | Expository | Explain why droplets form on a cold cup by connecting an observation to condensation. |
| Secondary | Argumentative | Use a poll of 30 volunteers to make a proportionate recommendation about a quiet lunch space. |

Choose **Start from Step 1** for a fresh attempt or **Resume** to return to saved work. The **Read**, **Draft**, **Review** and **Revise** tabs let you revisit earlier steps. You can write your own paragraph or load an editable example, then download your original wording, revision and explanation.
